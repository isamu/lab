SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between Acme Ltd ("the Client") and Beta Ltd ("the Supplier").

Section 1 Purpose
This Agreement covers the services described in Section 3.

Section 2 Definitions
"Deliverables" means the documents the Supplier produces under this Agreement.

Section 3 Services
The Supplier shall provide the following services:
(a) market research;
(b) a written report; and
(c) any other work the Client asks for in writing.

Section 4 Fees
4.1 The Client shall pay a fee of $50,000.
4.2 The Client shall pay within 30 days of accepting the Deliverables.
4.3 A late payment bears interest of 3% a year.

Section 5 Subcontracting
The Supplier must not subcontract the services without the written consent of the Client.

Section 6 Term
This Agreement runs from April 1, 2024 to March 31, 2025.
6.2 Either party may renew it for 1 year by giving 3 months' notice.

Section 7 Termination
The Client may terminate this Agreement if the Supplier breaches Section 4.2 or Section 5.
